## Selection boundaries / 收录边界

The homepage uses seven H2 directions: Real2Sim, Real2Sim2Real, Sim2Real & Sim–Real Co-Training, Interaction, Foundations, Harness, and Iterative Improvement. H2 headings describe broad research directions, technical routes, or system paradigms; H3 headings describe concrete methods within them. It prioritizes direct humanoid whole-body relevance. Each work has a primary table entry and may be cross-linked from other directions; contribution tags describe overlap rather than exclusive membership. The taxonomy is editorial, not an author-supplied classification.

- **Real2Sim:** adapt real demonstrations into robot-compatible simulation references, with contact-preserving motion retargeting as a concrete method.
- **Real2Sim2Real:** reconstruct simulation references from real inputs, train in simulation, and deploy back to real robots; PRISM uses counterfactual video augmentation within this route; Contact-UAN instead calibrates actuator dynamics using contact-consistent replay of real trajectories.
- **Sim2Real & Sim–Real Co-Training:** use virtual-source data for real-robot learning and deployment; distinguish asset/video-prior synthesis (GRAIL) from simulation-demonstration expansion (HumanoidMimicGen and DemoHLM), and distinguish transfer from mixed-data training.
- **Interaction:** distinguish learning from human demonstrations, dexterous whole-body interaction, tactile-guided manipulation, and contact/force regulation.
- **Foundations:** distinguish test-time command optimization, human-data-pretrained whole-body VLAs, world-model-conditioned policies, and generalist whole-body controllers.
- **Harness:** integrate perception, control interfaces, reusable skills, and execution feedback; distinguish autonomous skill orchestration from operator-guided whole-body data collection.
- **Iterative Improvement:** identify persistent execution-driven changes to later data, policies, reward programs, or capability retention. An iterative data–policy loop is not a claim of unrestricted recursive self-improvement (RSI).

The 2026-10-16 hierarchy revision places Real2Sim, Real2Sim2Real, and Harness at H2. Counterfactual video augmentation, interaction synthesis from assets and video priors, and simulation-demonstration expansion remain H3 methods within their corresponding routes. The broad “Data Generation & Policy Learning” umbrella is split into Real2Sim2Real and Sim2Real & Sim–Real Co-Training, while the former Systems section is named Harness. Prior distinctions among dexterous interaction, human-data-pretrained VLAs, and world-model-conditioned policies remain part of the collection. Existing explicit anchors and cross-links are preserved.

主页以 H2 表示宏观研究方向、技术路线或系统范式，以 H3 表示其中的具体方法。Real2Sim、Real2Sim2Real 与 Harness 位于 H2；反事实视频扩增、资产与视频先验驱动的交互合成、仿真示教扩增位于对应路线下的 H3。数据生成与策略学习贯穿多条路线，不再作为这几类方法的统一 H2。仿真迁移和虚实混合训练仍分别说明，保留旧链接与跨方向引用。机械臂、仿真人体及通用学习方法仍放入 [关联阅读](RELATED_READING.md#中文)，并注明适用边界。

## 2025-onward discovery expansion / 2025 年至今的扩展检索

Checked on **2026-10-17**, with a discovery window of **2025-01-01–2026-10-17**. This batch adds **22 core works: 10 first submitted in 2025 and 12 in 2026**, taking the homepage from 26 to 48 entries. It does not change the 13 related entries or the dated historical snapshot, and does not imply a new full audit of older entries.

**Discovery and coverage.** The official arXiv API query `all:humanoid AND (all:manipulation OR all:interaction OR all:retargeting) AND submittedDate:[202501010000 TO 202610172359]`, sorted by submission date, returned 794 records (271 from 2025; 523 from 2026). All four result pages were retrieved at offsets 0, 200, 400, and 600. This is a metadata candidate pool, not 794 full-paper reviews. Complementary arXiv-domain searches covered whole-body/loco-manipulation, force/contact control, teleoperation, contact-aware retargeting, bipedal manipulation, and test-time compute for behavior foundation models. The selected entries were checked against primary paper abstracts and relevant method/platform/experiment passages. This is a curated addition, not an exhaustive inventory of every eligible paper.

**Hard boundary.** New entries must contain a substantive contribution for a bipedal humanoid robot. Standalone arms, wheeled dual-arm platforms, quadrupeds, and human/avatar-only animation do not qualify. Human data and cross-embodiment pretraining are acceptable when the actual target and evidence are humanoid; simulation-only evaluation is not automatically disqualifying. Fame, citations, and project popularity were not selection criteria.

**Dates and resources.** Dates below use arXiv `published` (v1); `updated` is recorded separately. No verified earlier public release was found for the selected batch. LEGS is dated May 2026 despite its 2606 identifier. ULC remains a July 2025 work despite its March 2026 revision. Venue labels are omitted unless independently supported; TACT's arXiv journal reference records RA-L 2025. Some project requests failed or returned no readable content; those links are omitted unless a separate browser read verified the intended author page. InterEvolve v2 HTML was unavailable; its v1 full text, current abstract, and author project were checked. Its physical deployment replays prompts evolved in simulation with a closed-loop controller; it does not run the program search online on hardware.

**Classification.** The seven H2 names/order remain fixed. Six new H3s distinguish controller-aware demonstration alignment, reconstructed-scene data generation, visual policy learning/evaluation, tactile-guided manipulation, whole-body teleoperation/collection, and reward-program evolution. Existing H3s receive the other additions. One main entry per work is retained, including papers previously mentioned only in the historical snapshot. ## HumanoidTTT addition / HumanoidTTT 单篇收录（2026-10-18）

- **Identity and date:** [HumanoidTTT: Test-Time Capability Reuse for Efficient Humanoid Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00198), arXiv v1 **2026-09-18 21:44:30 UTC**. The submission history, rather than the 2610 identifier, determines the September date. This is a single-entry check, not a new full-list audit.
- **Embodiment and scope:** [Paper §IV-A and Fig. 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00198v1) identify a physical Unitree G1 using HoloMotion, with A–B–A motion-reuse examples covering walking, turning, waving, and squatting/standing. These establish humanoid whole-body control relevance, not demonstrated object manipulation.
- **Persistent improvement:** [§III-C–D](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00198v1) checks candidate-specific entry applicability before reusing a complete motion and uses Double-DQN to learn which qualified capabilities remain in a bounded store. Later successful reuse supplies feedback. Generation, tracking, acceptance rules, and acquired applicability certificates remain fixed. The learned object is the store-retention policy, not a new motor capability or a self-rewriting controller.
- **Evaluation boundary:** [§IV-E](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00198v1) evaluates finite-capacity consolidation through controlled identity-level replay over matched request streams with frozen execution-derived outcomes. Physical reuse demonstrations and consolidation replay results should not be conflated into a claim of long-term on-hardware learning. The title's test-time terminology does not make this TTCO-style command optimization.
- **Placement:** Iterative Improvement → **Skill Reuse & Memory Consolidation**, with a cross-link to Harness skill orchestration. This is distinct from InterMimicGen's data/tracker loop and InterEvolve's reward-program loop. Both READMEs contain one primary entry; counts change 48 → 49 and all seven H2 headings and back-to-top links remain.
- **Resources:** [Official code](https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/HumanoidTTT) documents method components and an application-provided generator/tracker/execution stack. It is not presented as a complete deployment implementation. The author project URL was unavailable to this browser check and is omitted from the new row.
